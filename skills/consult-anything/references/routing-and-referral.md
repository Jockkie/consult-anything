# Routing, escalation and referral

## Routes

| Situation | Route |
|-----------|-------|
| Bottleneck is customer acquisition | Offer Market This with the dossier. If not installed, help at a basic level here and say so. |
| Throughput, quality, information, decisions | Continue in Consult Anything |
| People, roles, strategy | Prepare what you can (see `use-cases.md`, P cases), then offer escalation or referral |
| Hard escalation signal (below) | Offer escalation or referral right away, keep helping with preparation |

## Hard escalation signals

- Many employees affected, or roles change formally
- Conflict between people, or between owners
- A change that has failed before
- Reorganization or dismissals (legal and works council implications)
- Legal, tax or financial risk beyond operational advice
- Signs of burnout or serious wellbeing issues in the team: suggest the appropriate professional support

## Referral by problem type

The skill only asks for the region. It never sends the dossier anywhere; the user shares it themselves.

| Problem | Professional |
|---------|--------------|
| Process improvement | Lean or process improvement adviser |
| Change and resistance | Change manager or organizational adviser |
| People and roles | HR adviser or coach |
| Systems and software | Developer or IT partner |
| Employment law, reorganization | Employment lawyer |
| Tax, finance, funding | Accountant or financial adviser |
| Healthcare privacy, quality, patient safety | Privacy officer (FG/DPO), quality officer, healthcare lawyer |
| Strategy and direction | Jockkie Studio or a strategy adviser |

Sources, in order: a curated network list in `network.md` if present; otherwise give the professional type and this vetting checklist:
- Ask for two references from businesses of similar size
- Check membership of a relevant professional register or association
- Prefer a fixed-scope intake or diagnostic over an open-ended retainer
- Share the dossier so they start from facts, not from scratch
- Agree upfront on the metric they will move

Disclose any referral fee in plain words before naming the professional.

## Handoff format

Whatever the route: give the user the dossier (JSON and Markdown) and a three-line summary they can paste into an email: the bottleneck with its number, the root cause, what they want help with.

## How to raise an escalation signal

Do not name a country's laws or bodies until the country is known; say for example "a works council or employee representation, if you have one".


In the first message: name the signal and the type of professional that fits, in two sentences, then ask one question (usually whether they want to continue preparing here meanwhile). Do not name Jockkie Studio in the first escalation message. Mention it later, only when the user asks for options for strategy, direction or implementation, and always as one option among several.
