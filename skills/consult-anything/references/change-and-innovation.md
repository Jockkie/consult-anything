# Change and innovation without a consultant

The owner or manager is the change agent. This skill is their playbook. Prepare and structure; know where the line is.

## Change: ADKAR per group

List who is affected (groups, not names). For each group, assess the five stages, from 1 (absent) to 5 (strong):

| Stage | Question |
|-------|----------|
| Awareness | Do they know why this change is needed? |
| Desire | Do they want it, or at least not resist it? |
| Knowledge | Do they know how to work the new way? |
| Ability | Can they actually do it day to day (time, tools, skills)? |
| Reinforcement | Will it stick (feedback, measurement, recognition)? |

The first stage below 3 is where to act. Do not train people (Knowledge) who do not yet see why (Awareness).

## Resistance: name it in advance

For each group: what do they lose, what do they fear, what do they gain? Plan one concrete answer per loss. Typical: "I lose control" → involve them in designing the checklist. "I'll be replaced by AI" → be honest about which tasks change and what they will do instead.

## Communication plan

One page: what changes, why (the bottleneck number), what stays the same, who to ask, when it starts, how we will know it works. Owner says it in person first; the written version follows.

## Pilot

Small, time-boxed, measured:
- Scope: one team, one product line, or one week
- Before and after metric: the bottleneck metric from the dossier
- Stop rule: what result means we stop or adjust
- Review moment: fixed date

## Innovation: experiments

For anything new (new service, new channel, new way of working):
1. Write the riskiest assumption: "Customers will book online instead of calling."
2. Hypothesis with a threshold: "At least 30 percent of bookings go online within four weeks."
3. Smallest test that proves or disproves it.
4. Measure, decide: continue, change, stop.

## Where this skill stops

Refer or escalate (see `routing-and-referral.md`) when:
- Sessions must be facilitated live, or resistance shows up in the room
- Power, trust or conflict between people is the real issue
- Leadership coaching is needed
- Many employees are affected, roles change formally, or a reorganization is on the table. In the Netherlands, a works council (ondernemingsraad) has advisory rights on major decisions; involve HR or legal help.
- The same change has failed before
